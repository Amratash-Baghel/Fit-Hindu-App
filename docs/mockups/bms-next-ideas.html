01Home heroEffort · M

The trinity, connected

Three separate rings read as three chores. One connected column reads as a path.

Today the three medallions float independently. Run a lit sushumna spine through them — the same chakra channel the splash already animates — and the hero stops being a scoreboard and becomes a body waking up, root to crown.

  • The spine lights upward as pillars close — glanceable progress with no numbers to read.
  • One line above answers the only question that matters: 2 of 3 · today's saadhana.
  • Toggle a compact concentric mode for small phones, widgets, a future watch face.
⚑
Rule kept: rings count workout · meal · meditation · jap · sleep only. Worship (aarti/darshan) is never a ring — it stays outside the tally, per the fence-line.

03DelightEffort · S

The day made whole

The best habit apps make finishing feel like an event, once a day.

When all three pillars close, the spine floods crown-up and a single diya blooms at centre: "Your day is whole." One earned moment — like today's blessing — that rewards showing up.

⚑
Rule kept: this celebrates the routine (body·mind·soul done), never worship, and it's never a paywall or a points-farm. A quiet aarti-lit beat, then it's gone till tomorrow.

04Home structureEffort · M

Home, re-stacked

Home is four full-width cards deep. Lead with the one question that matters, then get out of the way.

This is the answer to your own open question — "rings-only, or keep the devotional content?" A middle path: keep the soul of the screen, cut the scroll. The hero carries the day; one Next step card tells you what to do right now; everything else demotes or moves to where it belongs.

The verse moves into Mind's Daily Gita, where reading actually lives — so it gets a home instead of being a scroll-past.

05New screenEffort · L

My Path

The sankalp card promises a story. Give it a screen to tell it.

The streak card already taps through to /progress — but that destination is thin. Make it the reflective home: a weekly BMS grid that shows at a glance which pillar you keep and which one slips, the streak and its record, and the Fit-Points milestone track.

  • Per-pillar consistency, so guidance can be specific: "your Soul is steady; Body is where today's win is."
  • Framed gently — a broken streak reads as a fresh beginning, never as loss.
⚑
Rule kept: IST day boundaries, streak logic and points all come from the server functions you already ship (migration 0012). This screen only renders them.

06New flowEffort · L

The Sankalp

First run sets the emotional contract. Make it a vow, not a form.

A three-beat opening that ends on the awakening you already animate:

  • Choose your ishta. The deity you'll walk with — this seeds the whole devotional layer.
  • Set your intention. Strength · calm · devotion · discipline — a chip, not an essay.
  • Your rhythm. Days and time → mapped to a team-authored plan.
⚑
Rule kept: the mapping is rule-based (questionnaire → authored templates). AI generation stays scoped to the diet custom-plan carve-out only — nothing here generates health advice.

III

Cohere as one system

07Design systemEffort · M

One material language

The pieces are already beautiful. Make it obvious they're the same family.

Codify the vocabulary every screen is built from — the coin, the ember card, the embossed icon slot, the gold button, the 3D press dip and the haptic that rides it. Name them, document the one true light source and line weight, and no screen ever invents a one-off again.

Coin / medallion
Bezel + inset face. Every ring, every pillar door.
Ember card
The warm surface for verse, streak, blessing.
Icon slot
Embossed disc — one line weight, everywhere.
Gold action
The one primary button. Never more than one per screen.
Haptic map
Light tick on select · success chime on complete.
Pillar colour law
Saffron·indigo·gold map to Body·Mind·Soul — always.

Every idea here fits inside the rules already on the wall

English-first copy

UI in English; Devanagari kept only for scripture and deity names, shown alongside.

No health claims

Copy stays descriptive — "supports your routine", "energising", never curative.

Worship never gamified

Aarti/darshan is never a ring, never a paywall. Completion celebrates the routine.

Tokens only

Every colour above already lives in src/ui/tokens.ts. Nothing new invented.

Programs platform

Deities, plans and Gita cards stay admin-authored content — nothing hardcoded.

v1 scope

No payments, no community, no AI health-plan generation (diet carve-out aside).

A suggested order to prototype on the redesign branch: the two Effort · S wins first (living surface and day-complete) — they're near-free and change the whole feel — then the trinity spine and Home re-stack as the reviewable slice for the owner. My Path, the Sankalp and the muscle model are the deeper Phase-2 builds. None of them breaks a line in CLAUDE.md.
